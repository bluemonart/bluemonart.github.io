About

DigiBara is an adult management and simulation game. You take care of different characters across multiple rooms, manage their needs, produce resources and unlock content as you progress.

Play in browser

Features

  • Multiple characters with unique stats
  • Room management
  • Production mechanics
  • Equipment and modules
  • Save system (browser cache / export)
  • Regular updates

Status

The game is in Beta. Features and fixes are added often.
